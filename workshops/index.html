Workshop details and policies

Who it’s forThis workshop is designed for beginners and people with little or no cake decorating experience. It is a creative activity, not a professional training course. Written recipes and technique instructions are not provided.

LanguageThe workshop is in English only. You don’t need to be fluent, but you should be comfortable understanding and following instructions in English.

MaterialsAll tools, ingredients and aprons are provided. Please use them with care. You’ll take your decorated cake home. Tools, aprons and other reusable materials remain the organiser’s property unless stated otherwise.

Price and paymentThe workshop costs €45 per person. This includes the three-hour session, decorating materials, wine, juice and snacks. Full payment is required when booking to secure your spot. Spaces are limited and confirmed in the order payments are verified.

DurationThe workshop lasts three hours. Please arrive on time so you don’t miss any instructions.

CancellationsCancellations made at least 5 days before the workshop are eligible for a 50% refund. Later cancellations are not refundable, but receive a credit of 50% of the amount paid toward a future cake order. No-shows are not eligible for a refund or credit.

Photos and videosPhotos and videos will be taken for byamandasoyer’s Instagram, Facebook and website. If you prefer not to appear, please let us know on the day of the workshop.
