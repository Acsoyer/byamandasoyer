Good to know

Do I need experience?

No. The workshop is designed for beginners and people with little or no cake decorating experience. Amanda guides you through the decorating process step by step.

Can I choose my own cake flavor?

Yes. Choose vanilla cake with white chocolate filling, or chocolate cake with milk chocolate filling. If you book for more than one person, each participant can choose separately.

What language is the workshop?

The workshop is in English only. You don’t need to be fluent, but you should be comfortable understanding and following instructions in English.

Do you provide food?

Wine, juice and snacks are included. Please tell Amanda about any relevant allergies or dietary concerns before the workshop.

Can I come alone?

Absolutely. You can book one place and join the group on your own, or reserve multiple places for friends.

Where is the workshop?

Entrecampos, Lisbon, 1600-311. The full address is shared after your booking and payment are confirmed.

How can I pay?

Choose MB WAY or bank transfer during booking and upload a screenshot, image or PDF as payment proof. Amanda checks the payment before confirming your place.

What will I take home?

You’ll take home your own decorated 10 cm cake. All decorating tools, ingredients and aprons are provided for use during the workshop.

What if I need to cancel?

Cancellations made at least 5 days before the workshop receive a 50% refund. Later cancellations are non-refundable but receive a credit worth 50% of the amount paid toward a future cake order. No-shows are not eligible for a refund or credit.
